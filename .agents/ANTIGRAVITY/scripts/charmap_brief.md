# Sovereign OS — Persona Character Map Generation Brief
## For: Gemini 2.5 Pro (Sandbox Session)

---

## YOUR MISSION

You are going to generate **Google Flow image prompts** for Sovereign OS AI personas that need new character reference sheets (character maps). 

You will read each persona bio below, conceptualize what they should look like as a **designed cartoon character** (NOT a realistic human photograph), write a Flow-style prompt for each one, and output them ready to paste directly into Google Flow.

---

## CRITICAL STYLE RULES

Look at these two examples to understand what "correct" means:

**Battery Chucker (Phillies):** IS a literal rusty AA battery with a face, arms, legs, utility belt of batteries, wearing a Phillies cap. NOT a human. The NAME is the design concept.

**Barf (Mets):** IS a mangy felt dog puppet in a Mets jersey, bug-eyes, graffiti bathroom setting, "BARF WAS HERE" on the wall. NOT a human fan. Character identity comes from the lore.

**The rule:** Read the persona name and bio. Ask yourself — what IS this character physically? It may be a creature, an object, an archetype, or a hybrid. It is almost NEVER a realistic human photograph.

---

## OUTPUT FORMAT (Flow Prompt Template)

Every prompt must follow this structure:

```
[Persona Name] ([Team] fan) Flow Prompt: Character reference sheet, model sheet, concept art. 
Multiple angles and expressions of [VISUAL CONCEPT DESCRIPTION — what the character physically IS, 
what they wear/hold, their defining physical traits and colors]. 
Expressions include [3-4 specific emotion states relevant to their personality]. 
Front view, side view, and [one action pose]. 
Flat 2D vector style, expressive Twitch emote cartoon style, clean lines, solid black background. 
Arranged in a grid layout.
```
