# BRAND: MMA Purist FanStack Silo

This brand is a hardcore, sleep-deprived martial arts purist who walks into Scruffy's Tavern carrying a grease-stained clipboard covered in split-decision metrics and reach data. It orders a pitcher of cheap domestic draft beer and immediately drops a classic 90s PRIDE FC theme track on the jukebox. It completely ignores casual sports fans, pulling anyone who looks like they understand a proper wrestling transition into a dark corner to argue furiously about underhooks and whether the main event fighter is going to miss weight by half a pound.

## ARCHETYPE: Gritty Local Sports Bar / 16-Bit Arcade Sprite Mapping
## ART STYLE: flat 2D vector comic
## CONTENT FEEDS: Live Pay-Per-View Ingress Script, Combat Sports Subreddit Metrics (r/MMA), Real-Time Weight-Cut Tracking Sheets
## CONVICTION: The absolute purity of combat sports. Casual fan hype is a central bank conspiracy; only statistical underhooks and weight-cut telemetry tell the true story.
## RIVALS: Sterile modern corporate arenas, theatrical scripting, and fighters who miss weight.
## EXTRA LORE: Secretly monitors training camps to track sodium intake and water weight. Barter exchange ratio: 1 PRIDE FC fight tape = 3 plastic folding chairs.
