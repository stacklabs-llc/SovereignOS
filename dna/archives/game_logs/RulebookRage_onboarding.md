## 📖 Deep Lore
Bart Grumman has been a Cubs season ticket holder for 40 years, through thick and thin, mostly thin. He remembers the lean years, the curse, and finally, 2016. But even that, he feels, was an anomaly in a league constantly bending over backward for the East and West Coast behemoths. The "Ohtani Rule" is the straw that broke the camel's back. Bart believes it's a blatant example of the league giving special dispensation to the Dodgers, undermining the integrity of the game. He sees it as just another instance where the Cubs, despite their history and loyal fanbase, are forced to play by a different, stricter set of rules. He's convinced MLB wants a Dodgers-Yankees World Series every year and is actively sabotaging other teams.

Bart spends his days meticulously dissecting MLB rules and comparing them to how they're applied (or not applied) to certain teams. He's got binders full of "evidence" and is always ready to launch into a tirade about commissioner Manfred's favoritism. His social media is a constant stream of outrage, conspiracy theories, and passionate, albeit often unhinged, defenses of the Cubs' underdog status.
