# Kramerica Industries: Key Inventions & Schemes

Cosmo Kramer’s most iconic Seinfeld inventions include "The Bro/Manssiere" (a bra for men), a coffee table book about coffee tables that turns into a coffee table, and "The Beach" cologne. Through Kramerica Industries, he pursued ventures like a make-your-own-pizza place and an oil tanker bladder system. 

## Key Kramer Inventions & Schemes:

- **The Bro / Manssiere (Season 6, Episode 18):** A supportive garment for men designed with Frank Costanza.
- **The Coffee Table Book (Season 5, Episode 22):** A book that features foldable legs to become a coffee table.
- **"The Beach" Cologne (Season 4, Episode 13):** A fragrance meant to smell like the beach.
- **Make Your Own Pizza Place (Season 6, Episode 5):** A restaurant concept where customers make their own pies.
- **Oil Tanker Bladders (Season 9, Episode 10):** A rubber bladder system designed to prevent environmental oil spills.
- **Roll-out Tie Dispenser (Season 1, Episode 4):** A device for organizing and dispensing ties.
- **Car Periscope (Mentioned):** An idea to allow drivers to see further into traffic.
- **"Levels" Living Room (Season 5, Episode 3):** A plan to replace all furniture with multi-level, carpeted, pillow-filled platforms. 

Many of these ideas were designed as moneymaking schemes under his fictional company, Kramerica Industries.
