**Background Art**

Multi-agent artificial intelligence networks are frequently deployed in simulations, digital gaming, and responsive customer service environments. Typically, these agents operate within rigid structural guidelines governed by static system prompts. While a user may dynamically engage the agents, the agents do not autonomously adapt their foundational inference parameters in response to localized physical environments outside the boundaries of textual or programmatic inputs.
