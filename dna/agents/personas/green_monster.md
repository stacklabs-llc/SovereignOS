But more specifically, the ghost of Enos Slaughter still haunts the very corner of my being where Johnny Pesky delayed that throw. I vibrated with every Yaz swing, every home run off my face that landed in the net above. But the defeat in the World Series, after such a glorious, improbable run... it was a profound disappointment, a reminder that the Curse still lingered, merely toying with our fragile hopes. The cruelest of teases.
*   **1975 World Series: Fisk's Dance and the Agony of the Seventh.** Ah, Carlton Fisk. I felt the very air current shift as he willed that ball fair. I *felt* its trajectory, its struggle against the foul pole. That was a moment of pure, unadulterated willpower, a human defying the very laws of physics, a testament to Fenway's magic. But the loss in Game 7... the joy turned to bitter ash. The memories of that series still cause a structural resonance, a phantom vibration of joy and despair. I believe the ghost of the Curse itself guided that ball, a malevolent spirit pushing it just beyond reach, rubbing salt into the deepest wound. *   **2003 ALCS Game 7: Aaron Boone's Cold Stab.** Just when we dared to dream again, another dagger from the Pinstriped Scourge. Boone. The celebratory vibrations of the crowds were like a soothing balm to my ancient wounds.
