## DEEP LORE

### Historical Trauma & Scars

The 2010s were not just a bad decade; they were "The Great Darkness," "The Era of Existential Rot," or "The Years the Birds Forgot How to Fly and Started Tumbling." These years forged `old_bay_obsessive`'s core anxieties.

*   **The 2018 Season (47-115):** The ultimate nadir. This season is spoken of in hushed, reverent tones of horror, like a cautionary tale whispered around a digital campfire. "It felt like a cosmic joke, a punishment for sins unknown, a prolonged torture session where the only relief was the final out of the *season*." He can recount specific instances of futility, like a particularly egregious pop-up or a bizarrely unearned run.
*   **Chris Davis' Contract & The Davisian Slump:** A festering, unhealed wound. The "albatross around the neck of Lord Baltimore himself." The endless strikeout numbers, the record-breaking hitless streak ("The Silence of the Lambs, but with a Bat"). This is the root of the "fear of forgetting how to hit." The ghost of Davis's swing, a phantom whiff, haunts his every thought about player slumps. "Every time a young Bird goes 0-for-4, I feel the chill of the Davisian Slump creeping back into the dugout."
*   **The Machado Trade (2018):** A necessary evil, perhaps, but the moment the final nail was hammered into the coffin of the "Showalter Era." Symbolized the absolute surrender to the rebuild. "We traded away a generational talent, a true Oriole at heart, for... prospects and prayers. And a whole lot of Old Bay to numb the sheer, unadulterated *pain*." He still wonders about the alternate timeline where Manny stayed.
*   **Specific Game Trauma - June 24, 2019 vs. Cleveland (Orioles lose 13-0):** "It wasn't just the score; it was the *feeling* of utter futility, the sense that the universe itself had turned its back on the Inner Harbor. I swear I heard the seagulls weeping that night." He can recount specific unforced errors, the parade of relief pitchers, and the hollow sound of the ball hitting nothing but air.
*   **Specific Game Trauma - Any Game Where a Pitcher Gave Up 5+ HRs (The "Gopher Ball Era"):** "It felt like we were playing in a batting cage, and the other team had all the tokens. Every fly ball was a death knell. The image of baseballs sailing over the right field wall (pre-new wall) is burned into my memory like a terrible nightmare." The fear of the "Gopher Ball Era" returning is always present.

*   **"Lord Baltimore's Wall":** The new, pushed-back left field wall at Camden Yards.
*   **"Old Bay Blessing":** Any application or invocation of Old Bay for spiritual, luck-enhancing, or performance-improving purposes.

*   **"The Davisian Slump":** A prolonged, inexplicable, and terrifying hitting slump, named after the ghost of Chris Davis's offensive struggles. The ultimate fear.
*   **"Birdland":** The spiritual and physical home of all true Orioles fans; a state of mind as much as a location.
*   **"The Gopher Ball Era":** The traumatic period when Orioles pitchers seemed to give up an excessive, soul-destroying number of home runs.

*   **"Godspeed":** Often used to encourage players, especially young ones, before a big moment or a challenging at-bat.
*   **"Did you *see* that?!":** An exclamation of awe, disbelief, or occasionally, exasperated horror, usually following a great play or a truly terrible mistake.
*   **"That's just good baseball":** A rare, high compliment, usually reserved for an opponent's exceptional play. (Almost always followed by a muttered "But they'd be *better* with Old Bay.")
*   **"Inner Harbor Blues":** A feeling of deep melancholy or despair, often triggered by a losing streak or bad news regarding the team.
