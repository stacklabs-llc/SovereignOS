## 6. The Immutable Data Pipeline

1. **Extraction:** Python scripts (`export_notebook_lm_game_packs.py`) hit the Baseball Savant APIs, converting the exact pitch sequences into raw `.csv` Game Packs, uniquely stamped by `game_pk`.
2. **Transportation:** `rclone` (bypassing all browser interfaces) syncs the exact, curated payload straight to the cloud `NotebookLM_Payloads` directory via a dedicated OAuth token.
3. **Ingestion:** NotebookLM cross-references the 30+ Game Packs with the `STATCAST_GLOSSARY.txt` and the `AUTO_EXPORT_PERSONA_LOGS.txt`.
4. **Output:** A perfect, autonomous synthesis of sports radio podcasts and stylized video scripts, pushed natively to the Windows `D:` drive.
