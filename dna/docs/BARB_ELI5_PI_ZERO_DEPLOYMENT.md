# Barb's Guide to the Mini-Computer Project

Hi Barb! This is a simple, spoken guide to explain why James is setting up a tiny, friendly mini-computer at your house.

## What is this little computer?
It is a tiny computer called a Raspberry Pi. It is about the size of a pack of gum!
It is completely silent, runs on almost zero electricity, and will sit quietly in a corner without bothering anyone.

## Why plug it in at your house?
James is building a sports timing system. Think of it like comparing two stopwatches.
One stopwatch is looking at the live, instant sports data.
The other stopwatch is looking at the TV broadcast, which is always delayed by a few seconds.
By measuring the delay between the two stopwatches, the system can figure out exactly when things happen on TV.

To make sure the measurements are clean, the system needs to check from different houses.
Putting a tiny computer at your house gives us a second, clean vantage point.
It uses your standard internet connection just like a cell phone does, and it will not slow down your web browsing or TV watching at all.

## What about the mobile hotspot?
Sometimes, we also need to test how the timing behaves over a cell phone network.
To do this, the little computer might occasionally talk to your phone's mobile hotspot.
It only sends tiny, text-only notes, so it will not use up your data plan.

## Is it safe?
Yes, absolutely! The tiny computer is fully secure.
It connects back to James's home server through a private, encrypted digital tunnel.
No one else can see it, and it has no access to any of your personal files, computers, or passwords.
It is just a quiet little assistant doing some basic timing checks.

Thank you so much for hosting it! You are helping make the sports network run smoother and faster.
