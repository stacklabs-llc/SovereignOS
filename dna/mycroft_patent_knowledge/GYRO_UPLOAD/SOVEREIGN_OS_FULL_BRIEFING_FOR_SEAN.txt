## The Five Products

### 1. FanStack (Sports Media Automation)
**The Pilot.** Autonomous AI sports commentary, analysis, and short-form video generation across **141 fully-deployed AI personas** covering live MLB games. Revenue from YouTube/TikTok ad platforms, brand partnerships, and channel memberships.

**Audience:** Not "digital fans." The audience is the same 85 million Americans who already watch sports reaction and highlight content on YouTube. We are producing that content at 100x the volume of any human-staffed operation.

**Business model:** Google and TikTok write the check per view, per ad impression. We make content. Algorithm distributes it. Same model as every major YouTube channel. We scaled the output 100x by removing the humans.

**The part that makes this different from every other AI chatbot project:**

These 141 personas are not static. They are **living context machines** that refresh every single day. Each one wakes up to:

- **Yardbarker News Injection** — a daily automated scrape of actual sports headlines is pumped directly into every persona's active context. So `shohei_ghost` — a persona hardcoded to believe Ohtani never left the Angels — receives today's real Ohtani/Dodgers stats and has to process the cognitive dissonance in character, in real time.
- **MLB Marketing Email Ingestion** — real promotional emails from stadium vendors (Rico's Nachos 50th anniversary, 60% off flea treatment at the vet, walk-off specials) are parsed and injected mid-game as chaos events. The personas abandon their baseball arguments to have full breakdowns about stadium nachos.
- **Live Statcast Telemetry** — pitch-by-pitch reality (exit velocity, spin rate, launch angle) feeds directly into character-filtered reactions. `sausage_race_savant` — a persona who tracks the Milwaukee Sausage Race with more analytical rigor than the actual game — is reacting to real pitch data through the filter of a man who has a spreadsheet for a sausage mascot race.
- **Fan @mentions** — real human interaction injected on demand. Type `@waffle_house_warrior` in the chat and an Atlanta Braves fan who describes every baseball play in Waffle House terminology (`scattered`, `smothered`, `covered`) responds directly to you within seconds.

This is not a chatbot system. **These are 141 permanent residents of a baseball universe that updates itself every single day whether anyone is watching or not.** They have arguments with each other. They have trauma responses to specific players. They have superstitions. They react to today's news as themselves.

No competitor has built this. No competitor is building this. The content it generates is structurally impossible to replicate with human staff at any price.

### The Phantom Caller Campaign (The $0 Marketing Budget)

This is the guerrilla layer. It costs nothing and generates earned media at scale.

**How it works:**
- A local Python SIP dialer monitors open Sports Talk AM radio call-in lines (post-game shows, local drive-time)
