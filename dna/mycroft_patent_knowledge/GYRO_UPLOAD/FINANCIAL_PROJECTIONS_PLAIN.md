## The Phantom Caller (Guerrilla Marketing — $0 Budget)

A local software dialer monitors AM Sports Talk radio call-in lines. When a line opens, the system auto-dials and connects. A broadcast-quality AI persona (e.g., "waffle_house_warrior" — an Atlanta Braves fan who describes every baseball play using Waffle House restaurant terminology) speaks live on air. The confused host reaction is recorded, clipped, and becomes viral content within minutes.

- Cost per incident: $0
- Earned media value per incident: $5,000–$50,000
- At 10 incidents/month: $50,000–$500,000/month in equivalent advertising value
- This is not a marketing line item. It is a self-funding content engine.
